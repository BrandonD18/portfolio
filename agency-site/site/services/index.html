Services

Meta ads management and AI automation for high-ticket businesses

Take one service or both. Together they cover high-ticket lead generation from the first ad a buyer sees to the meeting in your calendar.

Meta ads management

Your Facebook and Instagram advertising, done for you by someone with 7 years of media buying behind them.

What you get

  • Campaign planning. Who to reach, what to offer them and how the budget is split.
  • Campaign build. Campaigns, audiences and ads set up in your own ad account.
  • Conversion tracking. Tracking set up properly, so you know which ads produce leads.
  • Monthly management. Campaigns run and optimised every month.
  • Funnel work. Landing pages and funnel optimisation where the ads need them.

AI automation

Ads create leads. Automation makes sure each one gets an answer. We build custom systems with n8n, Make, Zapier and Python that do the follow-up for you.

What you get

  • Lead follow-up. Multi-touch email follow-up with AI-written messages that stops the moment a lead replies.
  • Lead qualification. Leads are scored and qualified before they reach your team.
  • Booking. Qualified leads are booked straight into a meeting.
  • Lead reactivation. Old lead lists are scored and worked with win-back campaigns.
  • CRM and pipeline automation. Your systems are connected, so nothing is copied by hand.

Reporting you can read

You get regular reports in plain English: what was spent, how many leads came in, and what happened to them.

Who this is for

Businesses whose average sale is in the thousands. If one new client pays for a month of advertising, the numbers tend to work. We don't specialise in one industry.
